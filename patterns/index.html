Free Parametric Pattern Library

Curated hole-pattern templates created with HueScript by its community: halftone gradients, traditional motifs, dense technical meshes, and Fibonacci rosettes. Every template is fully parametric: open it in the browser, adjust spacing, size, gradients, and shapes, then export true vector SVG, DXF, or STEP files for print, laser cutting, CNC, or CAD. New community presets are added regularly.
