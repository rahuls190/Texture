Wrap a pattern onto a curved surface

Flatten the curved face of your STEP part, lay your HueScript pattern on it, wrap it back and export STEP.

Open Wrap Design a pattern Runs in your browser · Your STEP file is never uploaded

Start from HueScript

In HueScript, choose Export → Wrap: the pattern opens in Wrap.

  • To edit it, click the thumbnail and send it again. Only the pattern is swapped.

Click the face, flatten it

Import your STEP, click the face and press Flatten.

  • Planes, cylinders and cones: 0% distortion.
  • Domes and freeform faces: minimum distortion, shown as a heat map.

Place the pattern

Scale, Rotate and Move it on the flat. The 3D view follows.

  • Shapes crossing the edge become notches.

Raised, recessed or cut through

Pick Raised, Recessed or Cut and see it live in 3D.

  • Height, draft angle and fillets are adjustable.

Export STEP

Export → STP, in your part’s own coordinates.

  • Surface or Solid; Split STP for dense patterns.
  • For the flattened outline, use Import outline into HueScript below the flat view.

Questions

Which CAD programs can open the STEP? +

Any CAD that imports STEP (.stp). The pattern lands where it was on your part.

Is my STEP file uploaded? +

No. Everything runs in your browser.

Which surfaces can Wrap flatten? +

Planes, cylinders and cones unroll exactly; spheres and freeform faces with minimum distortion. Tangent faces can be flattened together.

Which pattern files does Wrap accept? +

SVG and DXF exported from HueScript.
