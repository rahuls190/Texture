Let your AI design the patternBeta

HueScript works as an MCP server: your AI assistant gets the same pattern engine as the app. Show it a picture, spell out the numbers, or just hand it a photo of your product. It builds, checks and hands back a design you can still edit.

Works with Claude, Claude Code, Cursor and other MCP clients

Give it a picture you like

A screenshot, a photo, an image from a mood board. The AI measures spacing, proportion and rhythm and rebuilds the look with the tools.

Or just spell out the numbers

No picture needed. Give it the panel size, the hole range, which way the gradient runs, the open area you are after and the corner radius it has to respect. The AI picks the arrangement, hits the numbers and shows you the result before anything is exported.

When the built-in patterns can’t place it

The same number of holes on every ring, every other ring offset half a step, rings closing up toward the rim: arrangements none of the built-in patterns can place. The AI measures your picture and tries the built-in patterns first; when none fits, it switches to AI Custom on its own, writes the arrangement rule itself and turns what is worth adjusting into parameters you edit in the app.

In the app you adjust the parameters of AI Custom; to change the arrangement itself, give the exported SVG or saved JSON back to your AI.

Questions

Which AI apps can use it?+

Any app that supports the Model Context Protocol (MCP): Claude on the web and desktop, Claude Code, Cursor and others. Apps that take a server URL with sign-in use the connector; the rest use a token.

Do I need Pro?+

Yes. HueScript MCP and the API are Pro features: every tool, from preview to STEP export, needs a Pro account. The app itself stays free to use.

How close does it get to my reference?+

Looks from the preset library are reproduced exactly from their recipes. For other pictures the AI rebuilds the look from its measurements, which usually gets close; open the design in the app to finish the details. When no built-in arrangement can place the holes, the AI switches to AI Custom on its own and writes the arrangement rule itself.

What can the AI access?+

Only the HueScript design tools under your account: previews, exports and design links, not your other files or account settings. Download links work for 24 hours; the files and design links are deleted after two days. Each tool call is logged with the tool name, whether it succeeded and how long it took (never your design, pictures or prompts) and kept for 180 days. You can revoke a token or disconnect the connector at any time.

Are there limits?+

Up to 120 exports per hour and 10 tokens per account. Reference pictures up to 6 MB and 16 megapixels; designs up to 200,000 holes. When your Pro plan ends, every tool stops until you renew; the app itself stays free.
